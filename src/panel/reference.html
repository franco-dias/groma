Inspect

hover
Box model of the element under the pointer, with a chip that names it.
click
Pin the element. The panel opens on it.
hover
With an element pinned: distances from it to the hovered element, in red.
right-click
List every layer under the pointer, to reach one that sits behind another.
G
Open the panel with nothing pinned, to reach its » menu and this reference. Again to close it.
Double-click text
With inspect locked on: type in an element that holds only text, right on the page. “Edit text inline” at the foot of the panel locks it for you. Enter or a click elsewhere applies, Esc puts the text back.
↑ ↓ ← →
With an element pinned: move the pin to its parent, its first child, and the element before and after it. Past the first or last sibling, ← and → go on to the parent's neighbour and into it, at the same depth. ↓ after ↑ goes back to where you were. They work wherever the focus is, except in a field or an open menu. Plain arrow keys stay the page's.
Inspect pill
Bottom right. Click it to lock inspect mode on, so none of this needs .
Esc
Turn the inspect lock off, and end a comparison. While you type in text on the page, the first Esc only puts the text back.

Copy

Click a value
Copies it. A size, a colour, a font weight: anything shown in monospace.
Click a token
Copies the token. Its theme path, its variable and its value in each theme copy separately.
Click “margin” or “padding”
In the box diagram: copies all four sides at once.
Click a component name
Copies the name. Each prop value copies on its own.
Click a class
Open the class list first; each chip copies its class.

Specs

Double-click a value
Edits it on the page: a size, a gap, a side of the box, a colour, a font size. Enter or clicking away applies; Esc cancels; ↑ ↓ change a number. A bare number means pixels, or rem in rem mode, where the field holds rem. A single click still copies.
Drag a number
Press on a value that is a number and drag sideways to change it live: 1 per pixel, Shift 10, Alt 0.1. A rem length moves 0.25 per pixel, opacity 0.01. Esc during the drag puts the value back. The whole drag is one step to undo. A press that does not move is still a click.
Tokens under a field
While you type a colour, a length, a shadow, a duration, an easing or a whole transition, the page's tokens that fit are listed. Click one, or use ↓ ↑ and Enter, to write var(--token). Esc closes the list first.
px · rem
Switch the unit lengths are shown and copied in.
Grid
Lay a 4 or 8 pixel grid over the element's board.
Compare
Makes the pinned element the baseline. Pin another to see only what differs.
Copy link
A link that reopens this page with the element pinned. Paste it in a ticket.
HEX · RGB · HSL
In the Colors block: switch how colours are shown and copied. The Text row also shows its contrast ratio and WCAG level (AAA, AA or Fails AA) against the background behind it; over an image or a gradient it says so and shows no ratio.
Copy CSS
Beside the element's size: copies the styles it declares (its rules and inline style, with your edits) as CSS, one declaration per line.
Asset
For an image, an inline SVG or a background image: its size and name, with buttons to copy the address (or the SVG's markup) and to download an image.
States
What the element's :hover, :focus, :focus-visible, :focus-within, :active, :disabled and :checked rules change, read from the stylesheets without triggering the state. Click a row to copy property: value.
Motion
Double-click a transition's duration or easing to edit that one entry; double-click “Transition” to edit the whole property. Animations are read only.
Effects
Shadows, filters and gradients, a part per row. Click a row to copy that part as CSS, the label to copy the whole property, double-click the label to edit it as text.
Pick colour
Pick any pixel on screen and see which token has that colour.
Breadcrumb
Click the parent's name to pin the parent.
Grid: Distribute
On a grid whose tracks leave room: where the columns (justify-content) and the rows (align-content) sit in the grid. While a grid is pinned, its track lines are drawn on the page.
Direction · Justify · Align
On a flex container: click a button to set it. Justify moves the items along the direction (justify-content), Align across it (align-items); the drawings turn with the direction. The last Justify button opens space-between, space-around and space-evenly.
Justify · Align on a grid
Where each item sits in its cell: Justify across it (justify-items), Align down it (align-items).
Columns · Rows
On a grid: − and + remove or add a track. Double-click a size to edit it; click the drawing before it to make the track fixed, hug (auto) or fill (1fr). More than four equal tracks are shown as one size, “each”, which changes them all.
Double-click “margin” or “padding”
In the box diagram: edits all four sides at once, as in CSS: “12 16” is top and bottom, then left and right.
content-box · border-box
Beside “Box”: which box width and height set. On a content-box element the width you edit leaves out padding and border, so it is smaller than the size shown.
fixed · hug · fill
Beside the size: whether each axis has a set size, follows its content or fills its parent. Click the word to change it.
Dimmed values
Only copy. The others also edit on a double-click, and light up under the pointer.
No token
A colour written by hand that no token has. Ask the designer before you hard-code it.

All CSS

Type over a value
Changes it on the page. Enter or clicking away saves; Esc cancels. A value the browser does not accept is dropped; “!important” after a value works.
↑ ↓
Change a number by 1. With Shift by 10, with by 0.1. A rem length steps by 0.25, 1 and 0.0625; opacity by 0.01, 0.1 and 0.001.
↩ on a row
Removes your edit and puts back the page's own value. Clearing the value does the same.
Enter
In the content box at the top: apply the new text.
Search
Filters properties by name. “Hide defaults” off shows every property.
Your edits
Are listed first, and saved in this browser for this page. They never change the file. See “Saved edits” under Edits for what happens after a reload.

Edits

Edits tab
Everything you changed on this page, on any element, as before → after. Click an element's name to pin it.
Copy edits
Copies the whole list as a text for a coding agent: each element, how to find it, what changed, and the token that holds a new value when there is one.
Note
With an element pinned: write what should change, in your own words. It is saved when you click away and copied with your edits.
↩ on a line
Removes that edit or note.
Undo · redo
“Undo” and “Redo” in the Edits tab. Also Z and Z after a click in the panel, outside a field. A reload keeps your edits but forgets the steps.
Show original
Shows the page without your edits; again to bring them back. They stay saved.
Saved edits
Are not put back on the page by themselves. After a reload, a bar under the tabs says how many are not on the page: “Apply” puts them back, “Discard” removes them. After a discard the bar stays with an “Undo” that brings them back, until you edit or pin something. If the page draws an element again and loses an edit, switch “Show original” on and off.

Layers and panel

Hover a layer
Outlines it on the page. Click to pin it; the arrow opens its children.
Drag the left edge
Resizes the panel. The width is remembered.
» menu
This reference, “Push the page aside”, “Show on a canvas” and “Collapse panel”. Click the collapsed rail to bring the panel back.
Push the page aside
On by default: the panel takes its width out of the page instead of covering it. Switch it off if a page looks wrong pushed, for example one with elements fixed to the right edge. Your choice is remembered.
Changes tab
On a Claude Design export: pick an older export to list the boards that changed.

Canvas

Show on a canvas
In the » menu: puts the page in a frame you can resize, to try the design at any width. Your choice and the frame's size are remembered.
Drag the frame's edges
Right edge, bottom edge or corner. The page lays itself out for the new size, media queries included.
375 · 768 · 1280
Phone, tablet and desktop sizes.
wheel
Zooms around the pointer. The toolbar also zooms; “Fit” shows the whole frame.
Drag or scroll the canvas
Moves the frame. Over the page, the wheel scrolls the page and a drag belongs to the page.
Space drag
Moves the canvas from anywhere, also from over the page. A middle-button drag does the same. In a text field, Space still types.
Inspecting
Works in the frame as on the page. Sizes and distances stay in the page's own pixels at any zoom.
✕ in the toolbar
Goes back to the page. The pinned element stays pinned.
A second load
The page loads again inside the frame, so it starts fresh: an open dialog or a filled form is not carried over.
A file opened from disk
The frame shows a copy of the page as it is open. If something appears twice or looks wrong, click “Use the file…” in the toolbar and pick the HTML file.
“Cannot be shown”
A link in the frame led to another site. Go back to the page.

Good to know

Sizes and distances
Are in the design's own pixels, whatever the zoom of the board.
Boards that look empty
Some exports draw a board only when it scrolls into view. Scroll to it first.
Props
Are listed as the design passes them. Check them against your own component before you use them.
